# The five challenges — Claude Community Halifax, 12 Sept 2026

Presented 10:00. Recorded verbatim from the slides. **Nothing chosen yet.**

> **Four teams per problem. First to the board gets it.**

Claiming is a race, not a deliberation. `PROCESS.md` budgets 10 minutes to pick — if a
problem is clearly ours, put a name on the board before finishing the analysis.

---

## 1 · Reporting What's Wrong
*Who the city never hears from.*

Only one in five reports reaches the city online. The rest phone. Some neighbourhoods
never report.

> The city fixes what it hears about. The people who can't report are the ones left waiting.

**Data:** HRM service requests · 311 call volumes · census

---

## 2 · The Same Blocked Driveway
*Same spots, every week.*

Ten thousand parking complaints in six months. The same spots, every week.

> Every repeat call is an officer's hour spent on a problem the city already knows about.

**Data:** HRM service requests, parking

---

## 3 · Which Tree Falls First
*Dangerous or cosmetic, same queue.*

Two thousand tree requests. Every one needs a site visit before anyone knows which is
dangerous. Two hundred and ninety are waiting right now. Some of them can hurt someone.

**Data:** HRM service requests, trees

---

## 4 · Is the Beach Open
*Tested daily. Nobody gets told.*

Every beach tested each morning for bacteria and blue-green algae. The result goes on one
webpage. Worst algae summer on record. It can make people sick, and it can kill dogs.

**Data:** halifax.ca beach status, daily · "from your chart papers on Tuesday"

---

## 5 · The Crane Is Waiting
*One person. Months of waiting.*

Every crane needs a street closure. One person schedules all of them. Builders wait months
with idle sites, and the housing behind them waits too.

**Data:** HRM street closures · building permits

# What the judges actually said

Three questions, stated from the front. These are the rubric now — weight them over
anything inferred:

| Question | What it means for the build |
|---|---|
| **Is this product usable?** | Not a demo-shaped toy. A real person could use it unaided |
| **Does this have first-day impact?** | Value on day one, not after adoption, integration, or a pilot |
| **Is the idea coming from real insight?** | Something true about the problem that isn't on the slide |

The third is the one that kills obvious builds. The slide states the problem; restating it
back is not insight.

## The demo is 3 minutes, not 90 seconds

**This changes the plan.** `demo-script-90` and the "90 sec" figure in `PROCESS.md` are
written for 90 seconds; the real budget is **3 minutes**. Roughly double the room —
but it is a presentation budget, not licence for more features. Scope stays where
`scope-60` puts it.

Delivery was called out explicitly: it should be **fun and engaging**, with Steve Jobs
named as the reference. Staging, pacing, and a single clear reveal — not a feature tour.
This is HA's lane and it is now the highest-leverage half-hour of the day.
